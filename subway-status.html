Train times, alerts and elevator status come from the MTA's real-time feeds, and dock counts from Citi Bike's public feed. All times are New York time.

Opened in a regular web browser, this page reloads the open tab's data every minute and updates countdowns every 15 seconds. Copies that can't reach the MTA directly, such as the one on claude.ai, show a saved snapshot of the subway tab instead.

Dimmed subway times are scheduled trips that haven't been assigned a train yet. "At this station" means the MTA lists the station on the alert. "No trains due" means the live train times show none for that line at the station in the next 45 minutes.
