Train times and service alerts come from the MTA's real-time feeds. All times are New York time.

Opened in a regular web browser, this page reloads MTA data every minute and updates the countdowns every 15 seconds. Copies that can't reach the MTA directly, such as the one on claude.ai, show the last saved snapshot instead.

Dimmed train times are scheduled trips that haven't been assigned a train yet. "At this station" means the MTA lists this station on the alert, and "Whole line" alerts apply everywhere on the line. "Not running now" follows the MTA's normal service hours, which are approximate and don't account for holidays.
